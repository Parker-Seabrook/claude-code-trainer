# Quiz rules

- 3 to 5 questions per lesson.
- 3 or 4 options per question. Options are short, plainly worded, and each holds one idea.
- Every question has an `explanation` that says why the right answer is right.
- `answer` must match one option's text exactly; the validator checks this.
- Wrong options are plausible, not silly.
- Questions test understanding of a scenario, not recall of a word.
